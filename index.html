Research Scientist · Google DeepMind · New York City

Joanna Hong

Speech, audio, and multimodal AI.

I'm a research scientist on the Gemini Audio team at Google DeepMind, where I work on speech and audio understanding for Gemini, from core model capabilities to safety alignment for audio-in and audio-out launches.

Before that I helped build multilingual LLMs at Trillion Labs, interned at Meta Reality Labs, and received my Ph.D. from KAIST with Prof. Yong Man Ro. My thesis on human speech understanding through multimodal representation learning received the Outstanding Dissertation Award.
